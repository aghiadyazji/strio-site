Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026. Applies to the Strio app for iPhone, Android and Mac.

In short. Your health data never leaves your phone. Strio reads it on the device to decide whether a habit is done, and keeps nothing from it but the tick. If you choose to sign in, your email address and your habits sync to a server in the European Union, and you can delete all of it from Settings in one step. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

Who is responsible

Strio is developed and operated by Aghiad Yazji, who is the data controller for any personal data processed. Contact: aghiad.yazji@gmail.com.

What Strio stores on your device

Everything you enter stays on your device in a local database: your habits (name, icon, colour, category, schedule, start of day, and any health rule such as "at least 8,000 steps"), the days you completed them, the pauses you scheduled and their optional reason, the reminder times you chose, and whether you have seen the introduction. Deleting the app deletes this database.

Health data

You can link a habit to a health source: Apple HealthKit on iPhone and Health Connect on Android. Strio then reads the relevant data type on the device and checks it against the rule you set. The result is a single fact, "done" or "not yet", recorded against the habit and the day. The readings themselves are not stored, not sent anywhere, and not used for anything else.

Strio may read the following types, each only when a habit of yours uses it:

  • Steps
  • Walking, running and cycling distance
  • Active energy burned
  • Water intake (hydration)
  • Sleep
  • Mindfulness sessions
  • Workouts (exercise sessions)
  • Weight

Checks also run while Strio is closed, so a habit can be ticked without opening the app. On iPhone, the Health app wakes Strio when new data of a linked type arrives. On Android, the system runs a check about every 30 minutes, once you allow Strio to read Health Connect in the background (you can allow or withdraw this in Settings and in Health Connect). These checks run on the device in exactly the same way as a check with the app open: nothing is sent anywhere, and only the tick and the time of the last check are kept.

Specifically, with respect to data from HealthKit and Health Connect:

  • It is read on your device only. It is never transferred off the device, never uploaded to our server or any other server, and never shared with anyone.
  • It is used solely to mark a habit complete. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, research, or to make any other decision.
  • It is not stored beyond the moment of the check. What is stored is the completion itself, which carries no health value.
  • Strio never writes to HealthKit or Health Connect.
  • You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app or Health Connect settings. Every health-linked habit can still be checked by hand, so the app keeps working without it.

Sync and your account (optional)

Strio works fully without an account. If you sign in, Strio keeps a copy of your habits on a server so that your devices stay in step. Signing in uses only your email address; a six-digit code is sent to it, and no password is created.

What is synced:

DataDetails
Email addressTo identify your account and send sign-in codes.
HabitsName, icon, colour, category, schedule, start of day, the health rule (the rule only, such as "sleep between 7 and 9 hours", never any reading), sort order, and timestamps.
CompletionsWhich habit, which day, whether it was ticked by you or by a health rule, and when.
PausesWhich habit, the days, the optional reason you typed, and when.

Reminder times and whether you have seen the introduction stay on each device and are not synced.

The server is run on Supabase, hosted in the European Union (Paris). Supabase processes the data on our behalf under its data processing terms. Sign-in emails are delivered through a transactional email provider that sees only your address and the code.

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. Settings → Delete account removes your account and every record under it immediately. The copy on your phone stays until you delete the app.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system. No notification service of ours is involved and nothing about them leaves the device.

Home-screen widget

On Android you can add a Strio widget that lists the habits still open today, with their names and colours. The widget is drawn on the device from the local database. Its contents are visible to anyone who can see your home screen, and nothing about it leaves the device.

What Strio does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking of any kind.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data.
  • No third-party code beyond the platform libraries needed to read health data and the client library for the sync server.

One technical note: while the app has few users, an automated request is made to the server every few days so that the hosting provider does not pause it. That request carries no user data and reads none.

Legal basis

For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom: health data is processed on your device only, at your request, and is not transmitted to us; we therefore do not process it as controller. Sync data is processed to provide the service you asked for by signing in (performance of a contract). Email for sign-in codes is processed for the same reason.

Your rights

You can see and change everything the app holds about you inside the app. You can delete it all with Delete account. Settings → Export your data gives you a copy in a machine-readable form: one JSON file with your habits, check-offs, notes, pauses and reminders, and your email address if you are signed in, which you save or send wherever you choose. It contains no health readings, because Strio does not keep any. For any other request regarding your personal data, write to aghiad.yazji@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

Strio is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. Changes that affect what is synced are also mentioned in the app.
