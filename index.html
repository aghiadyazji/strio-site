Streaks that don't break you

A habit tracker that logs itself from the health data already on your phone, and treats a day you did not do a habit as information, not a verdict.

  • The chain carries on around a day you did not do.Pause a habit when you travel or fall ill and those days are left out. Steady weeks earn freezes that cover the odd day on their own. The app never tells you off.
  • Your phone keeps the log.Link a habit to steps, sleep, water or a workout and the day marks itself done. The readings stay on your phone. Strio never sends a step count, a sleep record or a weight anywhere.
  • Optional sync.Sign in with your email to keep your habits across devices. Delete your account from Settings at any time; everything on the server goes with it.

Strio is in development for iPhone and Android. Read the privacy policy, or get in touch on the support page.
