Common questions

A health-linked habit is not ticking itself

Strio can only read what the Health app (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) lets it read. Open that app, find Strio under permissions, and check that the data type the habit uses is allowed. On iPhone, Strio cannot tell whether access was granted or refused; it can only see whether data arrives, which is why the habit says "Not seeing data from Health" rather than "denied". The habit can always be checked by hand.

A day I paused shows as done

That is intended. A pause shields a day you did not do a habit; it never discounts a day you did.

Deleting my data

Settings → Delete account removes your account and every habit, completion and pause stored on the server. The copy on your phone stays until you delete the app. Habits on a phone that never signed in exist only on that phone.

Sign-in code did not arrive

Check the spam folder, then request a new code. Codes are valid for an hour.
