Delete your account

Deleting your Strio account removes it from the sync server together with everything stored under it: your email address (and, if you signed in with Google, the identifier, name and profile picture link Google sent), and every habit, completion and pause.

In the app

Settings → Delete account. It takes effect immediately.

Without the app

Email aghiad.yazji@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Strio account". The account is deleted within 30 days, usually within a few, and you get a reply when it is done. A request from a different address is answered by asking you to confirm from the account's own address, so that nobody can delete an account that is not theirs.

What stays

The habits on your phone or Mac stay there until you delete the app on that device. Habits on a device that never signed in were never on the server. If you would like a copy first, Settings → Export your data saves everything as one file.

Common questions

A health-linked habit is not ticking itself

Strio can only read what the Health app (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) lets it read. Open that app, find Strio under permissions, and check that the data type the habit uses is allowed. On iPhone, Strio cannot tell whether access was granted or refused; it can only see whether data arrives, which is why the habit says "Not seeing data from Health" rather than "denied". The habit can always be checked by hand.

A day I paused shows as done

That is intended. A pause shields a day you did not do a habit; it never discounts a day you did.

Sign-in code did not arrive

Check the spam folder, then request a new code. Codes are valid for an hour.
